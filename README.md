Entry for the make.fun competition **Make the Judge Smile**
(https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d):
one line of at most 200 characters about a small good thing that happened
today.

## The entry

> A kid at the market dropped his ice cream, and the seller quietly handed him a bigger one, saying the first one was "just a practice scoop."

## How it was chosen

`main.ts` holds 12 candidate lines, each scored on the judge's published
criteria in the judge's order: on subject (a small good thing that happened
today), warmth that is genuinely felt rather than merely reported, a
surprise turn, one concrete detail, and fresh wording with no greeting-card
phrasing. Every candidate meets every other in a full round-robin (66
pairings); the winner took all 11 of its pairings — a small kindness, a
concrete market scene, and the seller's gentle white lie ("just a practice
scoop") as the turn. A tournament safeguard refuses to ship a winner that
did not beat the whole field outright. The program is deterministic (two
runs, byte-identical output) and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`.
Run it locally with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.
